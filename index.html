01 / ABOUT

Systems thinking, built from the field.

I am most interested in the places where mechanical systems meet computation, controls, electrical systems, and real-world constraints.

I am studying mechanical engineering at Oregon State University with a focus on the Computational Engineering option and a Mechatronics certificate.

I previously earned a B.S. in Sustainable Technology from Appalachian State University. During that program, I spent two years on Team Sunergy, contributing across both mechanical and electrical work on a student-built solar vehicle.

Today I work as a water plant operator for the City of Mount Holly, North Carolina. Operating critical infrastructure under regulatory oversight has sharpened how I think about reliability, process control, safety, maintenance, and the consequences of engineering decisions.

My long-term interests include mechanical systems, mechatronics, sustainable technology, clean energy, computational engineering, and nuclear energy.
