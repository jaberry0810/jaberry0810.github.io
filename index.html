01 / ABOUT

Engineering systems, from computation to hardware.

My current academic direction is mechanical engineering at Oregon State, with particular interest in mechatronics, computational engineering, sustainable systems, clean energy, and multidisciplinary design.

I am currently pursuing a B.S. in Mechanical Engineering through Oregon State University. My plan emphasizes the Computational Engineering option alongside a Mechatronics certificate, giving me a path toward mechanical design that also uses programming, modeling, electronics, and controls.

Before Oregon State, I earned a B.S. in Sustainable Technology from Appalachian State University. During that program I spent two years on Team Sunergy, contributing to both mechanical and electrical work on a student-built solar vehicle.

I also work as a water plant operator for the City of Mount Holly, North Carolina. Operating critical infrastructure has made reliability, process control, troubleshooting, maintenance, safety, and documentation very real engineering concerns rather than abstract concepts.
